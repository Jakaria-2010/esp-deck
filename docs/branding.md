# ESP-Deck and NexorOS Branding

## Names

**ESP-Deck** is the handheld device/platform name.

**NexorOS** is the operating-system name.

**Nexor** is intended as a recurring project-family name.

The project uses “Nexor” as an internal naming concept associated with
“next core” engineering. This is a branding concept, not a dictionary
definition or a statement of trademark ownership.

Before commercial use, perform an appropriate trademark and domain-name
search in the jurisdictions that matter to you.
