# Notices and Third-Party Components

## Project names

- **ESP-Deck** is the name used for the handheld platform/repository.
- **NexorOS** is the name used for the operating system.
- **Nexor** is used as a project-family naming convention.

Names and logos are not automatically licensed by the open-source licenses in
this repository.

## Third-party software

The CircuitPython libraries bundled in `firmware/circuitpython/lib/` are
third-party components and remain subject to their respective licenses.

Before redistributing a build, preserve applicable third-party notices.

## Branding

A modified build should clearly identify itself as modified and should not be
presented as an official ESP-Deck/NexorOS release without permission.
